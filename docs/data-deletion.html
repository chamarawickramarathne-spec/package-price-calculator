Request Data Deletion

Package Price Calculator — by Hire Artist Studio

App: Package Price Calculator (package name lk.hiresmcq.packcalc)  ·  Applies to all data this app collects.

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time. It is free, it does not require you to create an account, and we respond within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataHeld byOn request we…
Your sign-in email address Our server (database) Delete it completely. No copy or backup is kept after the deletion window.
Usage records (pseudonymous device hash, app version, platform, event type, timestamp) Our server (database) Delete all records linked to your device hash. These records never contained package names, client names, prices or your email address.
App content (saved quotes, equipment catalog, templates, business name, phone) Your device only — never uploaded Nothing to delete on our side. Remove it yourself: Settings → Apps → Package Price Calculator → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Retention after deletion: none. Once processed, your email address and usage records are erased from our live database and from our backups within 30 days. We keep no other copies and there is no grace period or archive.

How to request deletion

  1. Open a deletion request using this prefilled link:
    github.com/chamarawickramarathne-spec/package-price-calculator/issues/new
    (the title “Privacy data request — delete my data” is filled in for you)
  2. Include the email address you signed in with in the issue body — that is how we find your record. Do not include any other personal data.
  3. Submit the issue. We confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days, then close the issue.

Prefer email? Write to the address shown on our GitHub profile (github.com/chamarawickramarathne-spec) with the subject “Privacy data request — delete my data” and your sign-in email address in the body. Same 30-day response.

The Issues tab is public — if you prefer a private channel, use the email option instead. We only need your sign-in email address to process the request; nothing else in the issue is stored or repurposed.

Access request (copy of your data)

You can also ask for a copy of the data we hold (your email address and usage records) using the same steps above with the title “Privacy data request — send my data”. We respond within 30 days.

Questions

Full details of what we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy. For anything else, use the same contact channels.
