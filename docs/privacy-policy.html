Privacy Policy

Package Price Calculator — by Hire Artist Studio

Effective date: October 20, 2026  ·  Applies to the Android app Package Price Calculator (package name lk.hiresmcq.packcalc).

This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can have your data removed. The short version: the app has no advertising, no trackers, and never sells data. Your quotes and business details stay on your device. We collect only your sign-in email address and a small amount of anonymous usage information.

1. Data we collect

a) Email address

When you first open the app you are asked for your email address (no password is used). The address is stored on our secure database so we know who is using the app and so we can tell you about our other apps and updates. It is never shown to other users.

b) Usage information

When you open the app and when you export a PDF quote, we record a pseudonymous device identifier (a one-way salted hash of the Android ID — it cannot identify you), the app version, the platform, and the event type with a timestamp. These records contain no package names, client names, prices, or email addresses and are used to understand which features are used and to diagnose problems.

c) Information that stays on your device (not uploaded)

Saved quotes, equipment and template catalog entries, your business name, tagline, phone and address are stored only in the app's private local database on your phone. They are sent nowhere except into the PDF files you choose to share yourself.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No precise or coarse location
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta, etc.)
  • No financial or payment information (the app is free with no in-app purchases)

3. How your data is used

  • App functionality: to let you into the app, keep the catalog in sync, and generate quotes.
  • Service improvement: aggregate usage statistics help us decide which features to build next and spot crashes or failures.
  • Communication: the studio may contact you about this app or its other apps. Every message we send includes a way to opt out.
  • Security: usage records help detect abuse of the public catalog.

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL, HTTPS/TLS in transit) — stores your sign-in email and usage records. Acting as our database processor.
  • GitHub — hosts the app's releases and the contact channel. The Google Play build does not make any network calls to GitHub.
  • Google Play — distributes the app and collects the standard installation data described in its own policies.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Device identifiers are salted, hashed and truncated — the raw Android ID is never transmitted.
  • Email addresses and usage records live in a database that only the app backend can reach; the public app key is restricted to the operations the app needs.
  • Local data (quotes, business details) is kept in the app's private storage; Android auto-backup for the app is disabled.

6. Retention

We keep your email address and usage records for as long as your record exists. Nothing is kept "just in case": when you ask us to delete your data, we delete it (see below).

7. Your rights — access and deletion

You can request:

  • A copy of the data we hold about you (your email address and usage records), or
  • Complete deletion of your email address and usage records from our database.

To do either, open a request at github.com/chamarawickramarathne-spec/package-price-calculator/issues or email the address shown on our GitHub profile, include the email address you signed in with, and write "privacy data request" in the message. We respond within 30 days. To clear data stored only on your device, simply use Android's Settings → Apps → Package Price Calculator → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us an email address, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced through an app update and the new effective date will appear at the top of this page. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.
