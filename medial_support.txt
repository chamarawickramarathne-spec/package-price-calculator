3. LONG DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Package Price Calculator is a fast, native Android quoting tool for photographers
and videographers who price shoots for clients. Instead of rebuilding the same
quote by hand, open the app, choose the package, and let it do the maths.

Who it is for:
- Wedding, event and portrait photographers
- Videographers and content creators
- Studios that quote clients regularly
- Anyone who rents or bundles camera gear per job

Main features:
- Package Price Calculator: select equipment, set quantities, add labor hours,
  an hourly rate and a margin percentage.
- Live price breakdown: equipment total + labor total = subtotal, then margin,
  then the final total — updated as you type.
- Templates: save your usual gear + labor + margin as a template and start any
  new quote from it in one tap.
- Equipment catalog: add, edit and delete your gear with values, categories and
  types, so your prices always use current rates.
- Saved packages: every quote is stored so you can reopen, review and edit it.
- Taxonomies: organise gear by category and type for quick filtering.
- Clean LKR currency formatting (Rs. 12,345.00) and precise decimal maths.

Main benefits:
- Quote a client before you leave the venue.
- Keep every price consistent — no mental arithmetic, no forgotten items.
- Reuse proven packages instead of starting from scratch.
- Keep gear values and templates in one place.

Problem it solves: pricing a shoot is repetitive and error-prone. This app turns
that into a few taps and gives you a trustworthy, itemised total every time.
