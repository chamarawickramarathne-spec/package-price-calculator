--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LONG DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Package Price Calculator is a fast, native Android quoting and invoicing tool for
photographers and videographers who price shoots for clients. Instead of
rebuilding the same quote by hand, walk through the wizard, add your real costs,
and let the app do the maths - then send the client a proper PDF invoice that
carries your own studio branding.

Who it is for:
- Wedding, event and portrait photographers
- Videographers and content creators
- Studios that quote clients regularly
- Anyone who rents or bundles camera gear per job

Main features:
- Your business, your quote: enter your business name, tagline, phone, email
  and address once on the Business Details screen - every PDF quote then prints
  YOUR name instead of a built-in studio brand, with your contact details in
  the footer.
- One-time email sign-in: enter your email once (no password needed) and the
  app remembers it on your device, so every later launch goes straight to work.
- 4-step package wizard: Package Details, Equipment Selection, Labor & Profit,
  and Pricing - with Back/Next navigation and the final total always visible.
- Sensible defaults on every new quote: 3 event hours, LKR 1,000 per hour and a
  10% profit margin - change them in a tap.
- Live price breakdown: equipment + event hours + extra costs = subtotal, then
  your profit percentage on top, updated as you type.
- Extra cost fields that reflect a real shoot: extra gear, transportation,
  assistant pay, editing/retouching and a free-form additional cost.
- Two-page professional PDF: page 1 is the client quote (your business header,
  a highlighted package banner, a coverage summary, a green total badge and
  your contact line) and page 2 is your studio copy (itemised equipment with
  unit prices plus the full breakdown - equipment, event hours, extras,
  subtotal and profit). Automatic invoice number (INV-0001, INV-0002, ...).
  Share to WhatsApp or email, or save to your device.
- Project details: client name and project date, so a saved package is a real
  job record.
- Local storage: packages are saved on your device and work offline - open, edit
  and delete them from Saved Packages.
- Templates: save your usual gear + labor + profit as a template and start any
  new quote from it in one tap.
- Equipment catalog: keep your gear - with values, categories and types - in one
  place so your prices always use current rates.
- Clean LKR currency formatting (Rs. 12,345.00) and precise decimal maths.
- In-app updates: the app checks for a newer version and can download and
  install it for you in a couple of taps.
- Reliable sign-in sync: your email is stored on the device first and pushed to
  our list when a connection is available - duplicates and retries collapse into
  a single record, and your sign-in is never lost.

Main benefits:
- Quote a client before you leave the venue.
- Every quote goes out under YOUR business name - no generic branding.
- Charge for the real cost of the job - travel, assistants and editing are no
  longer forgotten.
- Send a professional PDF invoice instead of a rushed message.
- Keep every price consistent - no mental arithmetic, no forgotten items.
- Sign in once and forget about it - no password to create or remember.
- Works offline: plan and price on location, sync when you are back online.

Problem it solves: pricing a shoot is repetitive and error-prone, and turning a
price into something you can send a client is usually another chore. This app
turns that into a few taps and gives you a trustworthy, itemised total plus an
invoice - on your own letterhead - to send it with.
