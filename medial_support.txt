1. APPLICATION TITLE

Package Price Calculator

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
==============================================================================
A fast, friendly photography pricing tool for Android that lets photographers
build complete service package prices in seconds - labor, gear costs, templates
and ready-to-share PDF quotes.

3. LONG DESCRIPTION
==============================================================================
Package Price Calculator is a mobile-first pricing app designed for
photographers, videographers and creative studios who are tired of guessing
prices or juggling messy spreadsheets.

The app walks you through a simple 4-step flow:

- Labor: Set your rates for photography, retouching, editing and other
  creative work.
- Gear: Pick the equipment you will use from real-world gear categories. Each
  piece of equipment carries a value, and you can set quantities, so total
  gear costs are calculated automatically and transparently.
- Templates: Apply complete pre-built service packages (with Labor + Gear
  already configured) to save time and keep pricing consistent.
- Final: Review the complete breakdown, then generate a professional PDF quote
  you can share directly with your client.

Everything you build is saved automatically on your device. Category and
equipment data are synced from a secure cloud backend, so the catalog stays
accurate and up to date.

Main features and benefits:
- Accurate, transparent quotes: every number is visible and editable.
- Labor + Gear costing in one place - no more separate calculators.
- Ready-to-use templates for repeat offers and consistent pricing.
- Professional PDF generation for client-ready proposals.
- Offline-friendly local saving of your in-progress packages.
- A clean, distraction-free mobile experience built for photographers
  on the move.

Package Price Calculator solves the pricing headaches of creative
professionals: undercharging on busy shoots, forgotten equipment costs, and
hours spent reformatting quotes. It gives you a clear, professional number,
every time.
