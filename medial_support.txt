3. LONG DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Package Price Calculator is a fast, native Android quoting and invoicing tool for
photographers and videographers who price shoots for clients. Instead of
rebuilding the same quote by hand, walk through the wizard, add your real costs,
and let the app do the maths — then send the client a proper PDF invoice.

Who it is for:
- Wedding, event and portrait photographers
- Videographers and content creators
- Studios that quote clients regularly
- Anyone who rents or bundles camera gear per job

Main features:
- 4-step package wizard: Package Details, Equipment Selection, Labor & Profit,
  and Pricing — with Back/Next navigation and the final total always visible.
- Live price breakdown: equipment + labor + extra costs = subtotal, then your
  profit percentage on top, updated as you type.
- Extra cost fields that reflect a real shoot: extra gear, transportation,
  assistant pay, editing/retouching and a free-form additional cost.
- PDF invoices: branded with your studio name, an automatic invoice number
  (INV-0001, INV-0002, ...) and a full itemised list. Share to WhatsApp or
  email, or save to your device.
- Project details: client name and project date, so a saved package is a real
  job record.
- Local storage: packages are saved on your device and work offline — open, edit
  and delete them from Saved Packages.
- Templates: save your usual gear + labor + profit as a template and start any
  new quote from it in one tap.
- Equipment catalog: keep your gear — with values, categories and types — in one
  place so your prices always use current rates.
- Clean LKR currency formatting (Rs. 12,345.00) and precise decimal maths.
- In-app updates: the app checks for a newer version and can download and
  install it for you in a couple of taps.

Main benefits:
- Quote a client before you leave the venue.
- Charge for the real cost of the job — travel, assistants and editing are no
  longer forgotten.
- Send a professional PDF invoice instead of a rushed message.
- Keep every price consistent — no mental arithmetic, no forgotten items.

Problem it solves: pricing a shoot is repetitive and error-prone, and turning a
price into something you can send a client is usually another chore. This app
turns that into a few taps and gives you a trustworthy, itemised total plus the
invoice to send it with.
